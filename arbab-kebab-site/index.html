Menu

Middle Eastern food delivery in San Mateo & Malolos

Arbab Kebab brings the flavors of the Middle East to your table — chicken and beef shawarma, chargrilled beef kebab and shish tawook, fragrant chicken kabsa, mandi and golden rotisserie chicken, with our famous garlic sauce. Order online for delivery or takeout from our San Mateo and Malolos branches, and send your order straight to us on Messenger or by text.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we offer delivery and contactless delivery, plus takeout. We'll confirm the delivery fee for your area when we reply to your order.

How do I order?

Add dishes to your order on this page, fill in your details, then tap “Send via Messenger” or “Send by text.” We'll reply to confirm your total.

How can I pay?

Choose your payment method when you order; we'll confirm the details in our reply.

Do you cater for parties and offices?

Our platters are made for sharing. For bigger orders, message us on Facebook or call us.
