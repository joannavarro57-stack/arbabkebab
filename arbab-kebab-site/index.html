Menu

Middle Eastern food delivery in Malolos

Arbab Kebab brings the flavors of the Middle East to your table — chicken and beef shawarma, chargrilled beef kebab and shish tawook, fragrant chicken kabsa, mandi and golden rotisserie chicken, with our famous garlic sauce. Place an order online for delivery or pickup from our Malolos branch.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we offer delivery and contactless delivery, plus pick-up. We'll confirm the delivery fee for your area when we reply to your order.

How do I order?

Add dishes to your cart, enter your details, and tap “Place Order.” For GCash, you'll go to the payment page to pay and submit your reference number.

How can I pay?

Pay by GCash using the link after placing your order, or choose cash on delivery or pickup. Delivery fees are settled separately from GCash food payments.

Do you cater for parties and offices?

Our platters are made for sharing. For bigger orders, message us on Facebook or call us.
